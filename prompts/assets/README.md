The recipes in [`../recipes.md`](../recipes.md) are starting points; a different model or setting may produce a different image. None of the images contains a Vistella logo or a claim of product output.

## Imagegen prompts used

### Architect editorial portrait

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: Vistella GitHub prompt recipe example, portrait orientation 3:4. Primary request: create a cinematic editorial photograph of an East Asian female architect in an ultra-modern concrete studio. Scene: minimalist architectural workspace, soft morning sidelight through a tall window, faint dust motes in the beam. Subject: adult architect with natural expression and authentic skin texture, simple neutral clothing, no products or logos. Composition: medium portrait, one person, thoughtful gaze slightly off camera, strong architectural lines, negative space around head. Lighting/mood: natural diffused sidelight, restrained neutral color grade, subtle film grain. Constraints: high quality photographic image, plausible hands and anatomy, no text, no watermark, no brand marks.
```
