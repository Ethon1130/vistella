## 💡 Practical Composition Tips per Ratio

### 1. Square (`1:1`)
- **Key Advantage:** Equal visual weight in every direction.
- **Tip:** Emphasize geometric balance. Use words like `centered composition`, `isolated on neutral background`, or `circular framing`.
- **Ideal Scenarios:** Luxury packaging, minimalist ceramic vases, app icons, sticker art.

### 2. Portrait Vertical (`3:4` & `4:5`)
- **Key Advantage:** Matches natural human proportions without excessive empty ceiling or floor space.
- **Tip:** Leave 15-20% headroom above the subject's hair. Avoid placing the eyes directly in the geometric center; place them on the upper third line.
- **Ideal Scenarios:** Studio headshots, high-fashion editorial, street style candid captures.

### 3. Cinematic Widescreen (`16:9`)
- **Key Advantage:** Standard widescreen format that mimics modern cinematic film and television.
- **Tip:** Position the main focal subject on the left or right third. Let the background tell half the story.
- **Ideal Scenarios:** Concept art, architecture nestled in nature, narrative scenes with environmental storytelling.

### 4. Ultrawide Cinema (`21:9`)
- **Key Advantage:** Maximum dramatic tension and breadth.
- **Tip:** Include foreground, midground, and background layers to prevent the wide canvas from feeling empty. Use `anamorphic lens flare` or `shallow depth of field along the horizon`.
- **Ideal Scenarios:** Sci-fi megastructures, endless mountain ridges, cyberpunk highway pursuits.
